Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-23

This policy applies to the PC (Steam) and Android (Google Play) versions of games made by huart (currently Chef Survivor, “the Game”). The Game requires no account or sign-in, and the developer does not directly collect personal information such as your name, email or contacts.

  1. Save data
    Game progress (gold, ingredients, records, settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers (synced to Steam Cloud if you enable it on Steam). It is deleted when you uninstall the app, and the developer has no access to it.
  2. Advertising (Google AdMob, Android version)
    The Game uses Google AdMob to show rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one. AdMob may collect your advertising ID, device information, IP address (approximate location) and ad interaction data to serve ads, limit frequency, prevent fraud and measure performance. Where consent is required (e.g. the EEA and the UK), you are asked on first launch and can change your choice at any time in the Game’s Settings > “Ad Privacy Options”. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. How Google uses data. The PC (Steam) version has no ads.
  3. Payments
    In-game purchases such as “Remove Ads” are processed by Google Play Billing or Steam. Payment information is handled by those companies; the developer never receives card numbers or other payment details, and the Game only checks whether an item was purchased. Google Privacy Policy · Steam Privacy Policy
  4. Analytics
    The Game uses no analytics tools.
  5. Children
    The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
  6. Retention and deletion
    Game data on your device is deleted when you uninstall the app. Advertising data is handled under Google’s policies.
  7. Changes
    Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

    huart2026@gmail.com
