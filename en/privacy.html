Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-01

Games made by HuArt (currently Idle Mandate: Three Kingdoms, “the Game”) do not collect any personal information. This policy applies to the PC (Steam) and Android (Google Play) versions of the Game.

  1. Information we collect
    None. The Game requires no account or sign-in, and it does not collect or transmit any personal data such as names, contact details, location or device identifiers.
  2. Save data
    Your character name and progress are stored only on your device (synced to Steam Cloud if you enable it on Steam) and are deleted when you uninstall the app. The developer has no access to this data.
  3. Payments
    Purchases of the full game are processed by Google Play Billing or Steam. Payment information is handled by those companies; the developer never receives card numbers or other payment details. Google Privacy Policy · Steam Privacy Policy
  4. Ads and analytics
    The Game uses no advertising or analytics tools.
  5. Children
    The Game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

    Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
