Features

Quick runs, long-term growth

Four chefs

Four chefs, each starting with a different kitchen tool. Every chef keeps a record of the highest difficulty cleared.

Stages and bosses

The Vegetable Garden and the Butcher Shop. At 5 and 10 minutes, bosses like the Giant Pumpkin, the Cabbage King and the Roast Pig show up.

Your own restaurant

Unlock menu dishes with the ingredients you bring back, and spend the profits on chef training and permanent upgrades.

Star difficulty · Endless service

Difficulty from 1 to 5 stars, an endless mode that keeps getting harder, and a Daily Challenge where everyone plays the same setup.

Elites and recipe chests

Gold-trimmed elites drop recipe chests, and any recipe you can complete is offered first.

Cooking, not violence

You cook your foes instead of knocking them out. Bright, cute art that anyone can enjoy.
